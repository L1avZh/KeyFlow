# Support

- **Bug reports & feature requests**: open a
  [GitHub issue](https://github.com/L1avZh/KeyFlow/issues).
- **Security vulnerabilities**: do **not** open a public issue — see
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the private reporting process.
- **Usage questions**: open a
  [GitHub Discussion](https://github.com/L1avZh/KeyFlow/discussions) if
  enabled, or an issue tagged `question`.

KeyFlow is maintained by volunteers in their spare time. There is no SLA
on response times, but we read everything.
